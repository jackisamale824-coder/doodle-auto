How to play

Tap or drag on the felt to aim. Set the power, then take your shot. The guide marks the cue ball’s path to first contact and the direction each ball will travel.

Break the rack. The table stays open until a legal shot after the break pockets a solid or stripe. Clear your seven, then sink the eight in any pocket to win.

Hit your group first, then pocket a ball or reach a cushion. A foul gives your opponent ball in hand. The eight goes back on its spot on the break. Sinking it early or fouling on the final shot loses the rack.

Use ← and → for fine aim, and Space to shoot while the table is focused. Choose Play online to find an opponent or invite a friend.
